## The no-code discounts worth setting up first

Two of the channels in that table do most of the heavy lifting, so they deserve a closer look.

**MyLowe's Rewards (free).** You earn at least one point per dollar, and 500 points converts to $5 in MyLowe's Money usable in store, on the app, or at Lowes.com. Three tiers apply: Bronze (under $500 annual spend, 1x points), Silver ($500–$1,999, 1.25x, plus free standard shipping with no minimum), and Gold ($2,000+, 1.5x). Verified military members and first responders start at Silver automatically.

One detail worth knowing before you let points pile up: they expire 12 months after your last points-earning activity. An occasional purchase keeps the whole balance alive.

**The MyLowe's Rewards Credit Card.** New cardholders get 20% off their first qualifying purchase, capped at $100, and 5% off every purchase after that. The card has no annual fee and automatically bumps you to Silver status. After the first purchase, each transaction gets one of three options: the 5% discount, six months of special financing on orders of $299 or more, or 84-month fixed monthly payments at a lower APR.

The limitation that matters: credit card offers can't be combined with other credit offers, and you pick exactly one benefit per transaction. Also note Lowe's stores don't honor online pricing, so the same "coupon" can be worth different amounts depending on where you check out.
